Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 28, 2026

This policy explains what information cyberadspace.com handles and how. Questions: Contact@CyberAdSpace.com.

Information you send us

The project form on this site does not send or store anything. It prepares an email draft that you review and send from your own email app. When you email us, we receive what you include, such as your name, email address and project details. We use it only to reply and to discuss your project or inquiry.

Information collected automatically

Our hosting provider (Vercel) may record standard technical data, such as IP address, browser type and pages requested, to deliver and secure the site. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when your browser requests them. We do not run advertising trackers on this site.

Links to other sites

This site links to our brands and other websites. Those sites have their own privacy practices, and this policy does not cover them.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers needed to run this site and our email, or when required by law.

Retention and your choices

We keep email correspondence for as long as needed to handle your inquiry and our records. You can ask us to access, correct or delete information you sent us by writing to Contact@CyberAdSpace.com.

Children

This site is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

Changes
