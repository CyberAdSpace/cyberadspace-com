The Green Oven
Concept · Hemp pizza & shakes

Wood-fired pizza. Thick shakes. A little hemp in the mix.

The Green Oven is a brand idea for a small-batch kitchen: hand-stretched pizza with hemp-seed crusts and toppings, and real-ice-cream shakes, with hemp-infused options for adults where the law allows. The name, look and website are ready. It needs an operator with a kitchen to bring it to life.

The menu idea

Hemp pizza

Pies on a hemp-seed crust, with hemp-seed pesto and crunchy toppings. Everyday food that happens to use hemp.

Thick shakes

Real ice cream shakes, with hemp milk and hemp-seed options, plus seasonal flavors.

Adult infused options

Only where state and federal law allow, only for adults 21+, with lab-tested ingredients and clear labels.

Run The Green Oven

The Green Oven is available to an operator with a licensed kitchen or food truck under a private, written agreement.

  • Brand name, logo and visual identity
  • Website, ready for your real menu, prices and location
  • You run the kitchen and keep the large majority of what it earns; CyberAdSpace keeps a small license fee

There are no prices, reviews, ratings or health claims on this page because the business isn't operating yet. Any hemp-infused product would need to meet current federal and Florida rules, which change on November 12 and December 11, 2026.
